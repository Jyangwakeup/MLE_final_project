# Bound shared-parent Task 4 training and stop on independent failure

Accepted. The 24-hour campaign registered in `experiments/task4_campaign.json` evaluates at most A and the conditionally eligible B, with seed22 first and at most 300 rounds per seed, freezing each seed's first fully qualified 50-round checkpoint. Disjoint diagnostic/development/confirmation/main worlds and unchanged gates govern selection: three confirmations must pass before one main validation, and any engineering or independent-validation failure ends the campaign without reselection or safety changes. No new arm starts after 18 hours, no new evaluation batch after 23 hours, and all work stops by 24 hours with evidence retained; this supersedes the former Task 4 3,000-round/six-chain and phase-feature schedules, while packaging, upload and push remain outside this campaign.
