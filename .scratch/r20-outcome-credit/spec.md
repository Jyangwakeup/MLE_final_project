# R20 outcome-credit reward

R20 retains the R19 potential-based opponent pressure and the R18 safety and
anti-stagnation penalties, but removes positive action-contingent credit for a
predicted useful bomb. Crates and opponents contribute positive reward only
after the corresponding framework events are observed.

The Task 4 policy is warm-started independently from the frozen Task 3 r370
continuous-v5 checkpoint through the existing zero-extension migration. It is
not resumed from an R19 Task 4 learner.
